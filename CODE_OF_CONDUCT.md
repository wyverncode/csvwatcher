# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and professional. Focus on the technical issue, welcome
different perspectives, and accept responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will
be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.
